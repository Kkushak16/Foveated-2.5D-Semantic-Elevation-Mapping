    std::vector<uint8_t> ring_id;
    std::vector<float> intensity;

    }
};

🧠 No LiDAR Sensor? Qwen3-VL Maps Purely From Vision

Physical LiDAR is 100% optional. If a LiDAR sensor is not connected to your rover, our system automatically activates Qwen3-VL ("Sharper Vision, Deeper Thought, Broader Action").

Qwen3-VL inspects the live video feed frame-by-frame, performs monocular spatial depth reasoning, estimates clearance across 3 concentric safety rings (Near 0-10m, Mid 10-30m, Far 30-100m), constructs a dynamic BEV occupancy grid, and steers the rover along collision-free paths — achieving pure vision-based autonomous navigation without requiring LiDAR hardware.
